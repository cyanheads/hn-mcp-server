# 0.5.22 — 2026-10-18

## Added

- **`hn_get_thread` `item.storyId`**: a comment root names the story or poll its discussion belongs to, rendered as `story:<id>` in `content[]`. Absent when the story could not be found; `notice` says why. (#42)
- **`hn_search_content` `criteria`**: the applied filters plus `sort`, `count`, and `view` with defaults filled, `author` trimmed and `dateRange` as the exclusive UTC bounds sent, echoed in `structuredContent` and as a `**criteria:**` line in `content[]`. (#35)
- **`hn_search_content` `totalHitsExact` and `reachableHits`**: `totalHitsExact: false` marks `totalHits` as an estimate, and `reachableHits: 1000` marks a search matching more than Algolia serves; the last reachable page's `notice` names the ceiling and narrower `dateRange` windows. (#34)
- **`hn_search_content` hit `type`**: `story`, `comment`, `poll`, `pollopt`, or `job`, when Algolia records one. A poll option renders as `Poll option by <author>` pointing to `hn_get_thread`. (#41)
- **`hn_search_content` error reason `count_page_unaligned`**: a `count` above 50 with a non-zero `page` whose first hit begins no page of 50 fails with it, naming a `count` and `page` that start at that hit. (#45)

## Changed

- **Over-cap inputs are served at the cap instead of rejected**: `hn_get_stories` `count` up to 1,000 at 100, `hn_get_user` `submissionCount` up to 1,000 at 50, `hn_get_thread` `depth` up to 100 at 10 and `maxComments` up to 1,000 at 200, `hn_search_content` `count` up to 1,000 at 50. `cap` reports the value served and `notice` names the lever for the rest. Values above those ceilings are still rejected. (#45)
- **`hn_get_thread` `nextCursor` is packed**: a base64url varint encoding that also carries the resolved story. Cursors issued by earlier releases still resume, and a cursor longer than 150,000 characters is rejected as `invalid_cursor`. (#43)
- **`hn_get_thread` rejects a cursor it did not issue** with the declared `invalid_cursor` reason, so the rejection logs at `notice` with the reason attached. (#36)
- **`hn_search_content` drops a leading `http://`, `https://`, or `www.` from a query that is a URL alone**, so submissions under either scheme, with or without `www.`, match. The output `query` and `notice` show what was searched. (#38)
- **`hn_search_content` job descriptions** (`job_text`) are returned as `text`; they are not keyword-searched or highlighted. (#41)
- **`hn_search_content` notices explain empty results**: `author` is an exact, case-sensitive match (#39), and `tags: "front_page"` is the current front page only, with `tags: "story"` plus `dateRange` and `minPoints` as the alternative (#40).
- **`hn_search_content` escapes the query** in the `content[]` heading and no-results line.
- **Tests**: worst-case timing checks measure the test thread's CPU time instead of wall clock. (#44)

## Fixed

- **`hn_get_thread` returns live replies below dead and deleted comments** at their true `depth`, with `parentId` naming the omitted comment. The comment stays out of `comments[]` and in the notice's counts; where the depth limit cuts its replies, `notice` names it by ID to pass as `itemId`. (#33)
- **`descendants` and `totalAvailable` are described as live-comment counts** in `hn_get_stories`, `hn_get_user`, and `hn_get_thread`. (#33)
- **`isOp` on a comment root marks replies by the story's author**, where it marked the root comment's author. The lookup follows at most 64 parent links within 20 seconds. (#42)
- **A caller abort during a retry's backoff ends a `fetchItems` batch** instead of recording a failed slot.

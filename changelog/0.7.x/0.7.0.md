# 0.7.0 — 2026-11-13

## Added

- **`hn_get_thread` `item.storyTitle` and `item.storyUrl` for a comment root**: the title and link of the story or poll at `storyId`. `storyUrl` is absent for a text post, and both are absent when the story can't be loaded. A continuation page fetches that story once more, so `item` matches on every page. (#60)

## Changed

- **A page holds the count the caller asked for, never a byte cut** (#55): `hn_get_thread` stops only on `maxComments`, the 1,000-item ceiling, the time budget, a rate limit, or depth, and `hn_search_content` and `hn_get_user` bodies are always whole. The per-call caps stay at 200 comments, 50 hits, and 50 submissions.
- **Defaults**: `hn_get_thread` `maxComments` 50 → 25 and `hn_search_content` `count` 30 → 10, so a `page` sent without `count` counts in tens. (#55)
- **Breaking: output fields removed** (#55): `hn_search_content` `hits[].textTruncated` and the `highlightsTextOmitted` enrichment field, `hn_get_user` `submissions[].textTruncated`, and `size` from `hn_get_thread`'s `truncationReason`. Read each body whole from `text`.
- **`hn_get_user` `username` and `hn_search_content` `author` drop one leading `@`**: `@pg` looks up and searches `pg`, and `criteria.author` shows the name as searched. (#61)
- **Breaking: `hn_search_content` `author` that holds whitespace after trimming and dropping one `@`, or is `@` alone, is rejected as `invalid_arguments`** (#61); it returned zero hits before. `hn_get_user` rejects the same forms, which already failed there as `user_not_found` or `upstream_rejected`.
- **`hn-guide` skill 1.2**: covers the `@` and display-name handling, the 25-comment default page, and hits Algolia left unhighlighted.

## Fixed

- **`hn_get_thread` reports replies at `depth: 0`** (#57): `item.childCount` counts the root's direct replies on every call, dead and deleted ones included, and a root with replies now sets `truncated: true` and `truncationReason: "depth"` with a notice naming a larger `depth`.
- **`hn_get_stories` carries `stories[].rank`** (#58): the story's 1-based position in the feed, counted before omitted or failed items drop, rendered as `[rank]` in `content[]`.
- **`hn_get_user` and `hn_get_stories` count the items a page omits** (#59): `notice` reads like `3 of the 40 submissions in this window are omitted: 2 deleted, 1 dead.`, by kind (deleted, dead, missing), on final pages too.
- **`hn_search_content` page notice names `count` on every page it points to**, unless `count` is the default, and offers a larger `count` only from page 0, where it doesn't move later page boundaries.
- **`hn_search_content` `highlights` and `highlights.text` descriptions** no longer read an absent highlight as a non-match: Algolia leaves some bodies that hold every query term unhighlighted.
